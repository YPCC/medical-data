# Medical Imaging Challenges & Competitions

Historical challenge resources from the original catalog are preserved here rather than mixed with static datasets.

## VISERAL / Visual Concept Extraction in Radiology
Annotated radiological structures across CT/MR imaging.

- Historical access: http://www.visceral.eu/

## Grand Challenge
Platform for biomedical-image-analysis challenges.

- https://grand-challenge.org/

## DREAM Challenges
Systems-biology / translational-medicine community challenges, historically including digital mammography and somatic mutation tasks.

- https://dreamchallenges.org/

## Kaggle Diabetic Retinopathy Detection
High-resolution retinal images graded for diabetic-retinopathy severity.

- https://www.kaggle.com/c/diabetic-retinopathy-detection

## Cervical Cancer Screening
Historical Kaggle cervical-image classification challenge.

- https://www.kaggle.com/c/intel-mobileodt-cervical-cancer-screening

## Multiple Sclerosis Lesion Segmentation
Historical MS lesion segmentation challenge.

- Historical access: http://www.ia.unc.edu/MSseg/

## Brain Tumor Segmentation / BraTS
Multimodal MRI brain-tumor segmentation benchmark/challenge family.

- https://www.med.upenn.edu/cbica/brats/

## EEG Kaggle challenge families
Historical catalog entries:
- Melbourne University seizure prediction
- American Epilepsy Society seizure prediction
- UPenn/Mayo seizure detection
- Grasp-and-Lift EEG detection

Kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/

## MICCAI challenge ecosystem
Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention challenge/satellite-event ecosystem.

- https://www.miccai.org/

## ISBI challenges
IEEE International Symposium on Biomedical Imaging challenges.

- https://biomedicalimaging.org/

## Continuous Registration Challenge
Historical SuperElastix registration challenge over lung and brain images.

- https://continuousregistration.grand-challenge.org/

## MURA
Musculoskeletal radiograph abnormality detection benchmark.

- https://stanfordmlgroup.github.io/competitions/mura/
