# TREC Clinical Decision Support / Precision Medicine

The Text REtrieval Conference (TREC) has hosted clinical decision-support and precision-medicine retrieval tracks.

## Historical Clinical Decision Support tracks

### 2014
Retrieval of biomedical literature relevant to generic clinical questions derived from medical cases.

- Historical information: http://www.trec-cds.org/2014.html

### 2015
Biomedical literature retrieval for clinical decision support.

- Historical information: http://www.trec-cds.org/2015.html

### 2016
Clinical decision-support retrieval using EHR-derived patient records rather than only synthetic cases.

- Historical information: http://www.trec-cds.org/2016.html

### 2017 Precision Medicine
Retrieval of precision-medicine information relevant to oncology patients.

- Historical information: http://www.trec-cds.org/2017.html
