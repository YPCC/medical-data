# National & Public Healthcare Data

## CDC
U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention datasets covering health statistics, surveillance, vaccination, injury, tobacco, NCHS and other public-health domains.

- Landing page: https://data.cdc.gov/
- Catalog: https://data.cdc.gov/browse
- `modality:population-data` `access:open-or-dataset-specific`

## CMS / Medicare data
Data on hospitals, nursing homes, physicians, home health, dialysis, providers and other U.S. healthcare topics.

- Current CMS data portal: https://data.cms.gov/
- Medicare: https://www.medicare.gov/care-compare/
- `modality:administrative-data`

## Texas Public Use Inpatient Data File
State inpatient public-use data with diagnoses, procedures and outcomes.

- Information: https://www.dshs.texas.gov/thcic/hospitals/Inpatientpudf.shtm
- `modality:administrative-data`

## Dollars for Doctors
ProPublica investigation and related data concerning pharmaceutical-company payments to physicians.

- Information: https://www.propublica.org/series/dollars-for-docs
- Search: https://projects.propublica.org/docdollars/
- `modality:public-data`

## DocGraph
Historical physician-interaction/referral-network resource retained from the original catalog.

- Historical main page: http://www.docgraph.com/
- Historical information: http://thehealthcareblog.com/blog/2012/11/05/tracking-the-social-doctor-opening-up-physician-referral-data-and-much-more/
- `modality:network-data`
