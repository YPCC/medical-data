# Biomedical Literature

## PMC Open Access Subset
Full-text open-access articles in PubMed Central subject to the licenses associated with each article.

- Information: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/tools/openftlist/
- Download / data mining: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/tools/ftp/
- `modality:biomedical-literature` `access:open-or-license-specific`

## PubMed 200k RCT
PubMed randomized-controlled-trial abstracts with sentence-level rhetorical-role annotations.

- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.06071
- Data: https://github.com/Franck-Dernoncourt/pubmed-rct
- `modality:abstract` `task:sentence-classification`

## BioC PubMed / PMC APIs
NLM BioC interfaces for retrieving PubMed abstracts and PubMed Central full text in a structured interchange format.

- PubMed: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/bionlp/APIs/BioC-PubMed/
- PMC: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/bionlp/APIs/BioC-PMC/
- `format:bioc`

For annotated biomedical NER/relation corpora, see [Biomedical NLP](biomedical-nlp/README.md).
