# Medical Speech Data

## TORGO
Acoustic and 3D articulatory recordings from speakers with dysarthria associated with cerebral palsy or ALS, plus matched controls.

- Historical information/data: http://www.cs.toronto.edu/~complingweb/data/TORGO/torgo.html
- `modality:speech` `domain:dysarthria`

## NKI-CCRT Corpus
Speech intelligibility recordings and listener judgments before and after treatment for advanced head and neck cancer.

- Paper: http://lrec.elra.info/proceedings/lrec2012/pdf/230_Paper.pdf
- Access: contact authors / restricted scientific use
- `modality:speech` `access:request`

## EMOTASS / Atypical Affect Interspeech Sub-Challenge
Affective speech from disabled adults for emotion classification.

- Historical paper: http://emotion-research.net/sigs/speech-sig/is2018_compare.pdf
- Historical challenge link: http://emotion-research.net/sigs/speech-sig/is18-compare
- `modality:speech` `task:emotion-classification`

## Autism Sub-Challenge / Child Pathological Speech Database
Speech recordings from children used in an Interspeech computational paralinguistics challenge.

- Historical paper: http://emotion-research.net/sigs/speech-sig/is2013_compare.pdf
- Historical challenge link: http://emotion-research.net/sigs/speech-sig/is13-compare
- `modality:speech` `domain:autism`
